For My Appa ❤️

Appa,

On this Teachers' Day, I just want to tell you something that I probably don't say often enough.

Thank you for being my first teacher, my guide, and the person who has always stood by me no matter what. Whenever life became difficult, you were there. Whenever I made mistakes, you didn't judge me—you helped me understand them, learn from them, and become better.

The lessons you have taught me go far beyond studies. Through your actions, you showed me the importance of hard work, discipline, honesty, kindness, and helping others without expecting anything in return.

One thing that has always inspired me is your dedication to your students. For more than 10 years, you have spent your summer holidays teaching 10th-grade students for free, giving your time and knowledge to help them build a better future. Seeing the difference you have made in so many lives makes me proud to be your child.

Appa, you are much more than a teacher to me. You are my role model, my strength, and the person I look up to every day.

I know I haven't made you as proud as I want to yet. There are times when I feel I could have done better. But today, I want to make you a promise. One day, I will make you proud in a way that reflects all the sacrifices, love, and values you have given me. It may not be today, and it may not be tomorrow, but I will keep working until that day comes.

Thank you for believing in me even when I struggle to believe in myself.

You are the greatest father I could ever ask for, Appa.

Happy Teachers' Day. ❤️

With all my love and gratitude,
Your child ❤️
